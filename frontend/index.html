VariantLens runs a BRCA1 / BRCA2 query through four cooperating agents — intake, evidence retrieval, classification and reporting — and shows its working. Every verdict arrives with the evidence that produced it, the ACMG-style criteria applied, and a place for a qualified human to sign off.

Free plan: 10 queries a month, rule-based reasoning, no credit card.

01

Four agents, one verdict

Intake extracts gene, HGVS, position and rsID. Evidence retrieval ranks real ClinVar records. Classification applies ACMG-style rules. Reporting explains it in plain language.

02

Evidence you can check

Top-ranked variants with relevance scores, the criteria applied, and the ClinVar consensus behind the call — not a bare verdict.

03

Built for human oversight

Uncertain calls are labelled as such. Nothing reaches a report without a reviewer, date and signature, stored with the reviewing account.

04

Honest about its limits

Population-frequency evidence is flagged as limited. Decision support, never a diagnosis. Every result carries that framing.
